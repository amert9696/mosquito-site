Support

For help with Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run, email alimertgulec38@gmail.com. We answer within 2 business days. Please include your device model and, for purchases, the date of the purchase. Most questions are answered below.

My purchase did not arrive

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Non-consumable items, No Ads and the Mosquito Club come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Blood packs and other consumables cannot be restored. If something is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I cancel the Mosquito Club?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mosquito. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the game does not cancel it.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

In the game: Settings → Delete account (needs an internet connection). Or email us from the address you want answered with the subject “Delete my data” and the Player ID shown in the game under Profile. We delete your cloud save and profile within 30 days.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone and backed up to the cloud with an anonymous Player ID. To move it to a new phone, link your account in Settings → Link Apple / Google before you switch. If it is gone, email us your Player ID and we will check the backup.

Online says it cannot reach the servers

When the online servers cannot be reached, the match runs against bots on your phone so you can still play. Check your connection and try again; if it keeps happening, email us the time it happened.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, the night or screen where it happened and, if you can, a screenshot or screen recording.

I won the Game of the Week

Congratulations! Tap Claim your prize on the champion card in the game. It opens an email to us with the week and your claim code in the subject; send it as it is.
