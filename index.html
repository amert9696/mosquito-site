Support · Destek

Email: alimertgulec38@gmail.com. We answer within 2 business days. Please include your device model and, for purchases, the date of the purchase.

Purchases did not arrive / Satın aldığım ürün gelmedi

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Non-consumable items, No Ads and the Mosquito Club come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Blood packs and other consumables cannot be restored.

Oyunda Ayarlar → Satın alımları geri yükle. Kalıcı ürünler, Reklamsız ve Mosquito Kulübü aynı Apple Hesabı ile her cihazda geri gelir. Kan paketleri gibi tüketilebilir ürünler geri yüklenemez.

Cancel the Mosquito Club / Kulüp aboneliğini iptal

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mosquito. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

iPhone Ayarlar → adınız → Abonelikler → Mosquito. Yenileme tarihinden en az 24 saat önce iptal edin.

Refunds / İade

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my data / Verilerimi sil

Email us from the address you want answered with the subject “Delete my data” and the Player ID shown in the game under Profile. We delete your cloud save and profile within 30 days.
