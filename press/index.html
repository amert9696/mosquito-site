Short description (40 words)

Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run is a free iPhone stealth game where you play as a mosquito: sneak through 20 realistic rooms, land the perfect bite on sleeping people, dodge the slap in slow motion and escape before dawn, solo or online.

Long description

You are a mosquito. They are asleep. Mostly. Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run follows one mosquito through one summer: 20 nights in real places at insect scale, from a bedroom to a baby's room, an exam hall, a camping tent, a cinema and the metro. Sleepers breathe out visible warm plumes that lead you to skin, every sip makes you heavier, and a perfect “golden” bite is a matter of letting go at the right moment. When a hand comes down, time slows, and a well-timed dash saves you and a shareable clip.

Beyond the story there is a Daily Night that is the same for everyone, a ranked Game of the Week, an endless mode and Online 1 vs 3, in which one player is the human and three are mosquitoes. The ten playable mosquitoes are real species, from the house mosquito to the iridescent Sabethes, each with a bonus based on its biology. There is no music track: a live synth plays your flight.
