How to play Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run

You win a night by drinking enough blood to open the window and then flying out before dawn. Hold anywhere on the screen to fly and drag to steer; when you are next to bare skin a BITE button appears. Hold it to drink and let go before the ring turns red, or the sleeper feels the itch and swats.

What are the controls?

The default scheme, One finger, needs a single thumb. Settings also offer Two thumbs and Classic stick schemes.

ActionTouch control
FlyHold anywhere. Holding is your throttle.
SteerDrag left or right to turn, up or down to climb or dive.
HoverLet go. You brake and hang in the air.
LandHold still close to a surface or to skin.
DashTap DASH. You have 3 charges that refill over time.
BiteHold BITE when it appears next to skin; release to stop drinking.

In Settings you can change flight speed, sensitivity and camera distance, switch on gyro assist, invert vertical steering, move the buttons for left-handed play, turn on reduced motion, pick a colour-blind thermal palette and set graphics quality and frame rate.

How does a night work, step by step?

  1. Read the briefing. It names the room, the blood target and the star challenges. Early nights each teach one new idea, marked NEW.
  2. Find a sleeper. The top bar shows how awake the room is. Light, noise and open eyes raise it.
  3. Drink. Land on exposed skin and hold BITE. The blood meter at the top left fills toward the target.
  4. Wait for the window. When you reach the target the window opens with a glow and a beam of moonlight. Before that it refuses you.
  5. Fly out before dawn. The dawn clock in the corner counts down. Only flying out of the window finishes the night.

How do you land a perfect bite?

A bite is a short timing game. While you drink, a ring fills around the button. Just before the itch arrives, the ring turns gold: let go then and the bite pays 25% more blood. If you hold on until the ring turns red, the sleeper feels it and reacts.

  • Clean streaks: each bite in a row that ends before the red and draws no swing pays more, up to +60%.
  • Blood tastes: you are what they ate. Coffee makes you fast, sugar fills you quicker, wine pays more but makes your flight wobble.
  • Welts: the bitten spot swells on the skin, so you can see where you have already been.

What are the breath plumes?

Sleepers breathe out warm plumes you can see. Fly inside one to lock onto the scent: it guides you to skin and makes the next bites pay up to 50% more. Real mosquitoes do something similar with exhaled carbon dioxide, as our article on how mosquitoes find people explains.

Why does the mosquito get slow?

Blood has weight. Each sip swells your belly, slows your flight and makes turns wider. A full mosquito is an easy target, so plan your exit before the last bite.

How do people fight back?

  • Slaps: a hand coming down triggers Slap Time, a slow-motion moment in which a dash can still save you. The game records the near miss as a clip you can save and share.
  • Rolling over: a sleeper bitten while stirring rolls over and covers the bitten spot for 18 seconds.
  • Sneezing: hover at a nose for a second and you get sneezed across the room.
  • Bug spray: from night 08, someone who loses you twice fogs the spot with spray. Get out of the cloud.
  • Bosses: night 10 is the Racket Uncle with an electric racket; night 20 is Pest Control.

Hiding works: when someone searches for you, land on a dark wall or curtain and keep still. They look right past you.

How are stars earned?

Each night has three stars, shown on the results card:

StarHow to earn it
Blood targetReach the night's blood target and escape.
Chaos or ghostEither trigger the room's chaos or escape without being seen.
Golden momentsLand the required number of golden bites.

Tips for your first nights

  1. Start every night by finding a breath plume. It saves time and pays more.
  2. Bite feet and hands first: they are far from the eyes.
  3. Keep one dash charge for the escape. Slap Time only helps if you can dash.
  4. Do not chase the last drop. Leave with the target, not with a full tank.
  5. Lost? A small chevron on screen points to the nearest exposed skin, or to the window once it is open.
