Mosquito facts and guides

These guides explain the real biology behind Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run, with every claim linked to a study, the CDC or a university source. Each one also shows exactly where the game follows the science and where it bends it for fun.

  • How do mosquitoes find you? CO₂, heat and scentFour studies from 2014 to 2024 on breath, sight, body heat and skin odour, and how the game's breath plumes model them.
  • Why do mosquito bites itch?What is in mosquito saliva, why only females bite, what helps, and how the game's bite ring uses the itch as a clock.
  • Games where you play as an insectFrom Mister Mosquito (2001) to 1-vs-4 mosquito PvP on Steam: platforms, years and what you do.

Looking for mosquito species?

The species page covers ten real mosquitoes, from the house mosquito to the elephant mosquito, with sources for each.

How these guides are written

The developer of the game writes and checks each guide. Facts come from peer-reviewed studies, public-health agencies and university extension services, linked inline and listed at the end. Game details come from the game's own data. If you spot an error, write to us via the support page and we will correct it and update the date.
