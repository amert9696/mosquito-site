Privacy Policy

Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run · Last updated 28 October 2026 · Türkçe

Mosquito is made by Ali Mert Güleç (“we”). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: alimertgulec38@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Game progress (levels, stars, Blood, items, settings) is stored on your device. If cloud save is available it is also stored with an anonymous Player ID on our backend (Supabase, EU/US servers) so it can be restored. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number in the game.
  • Online play: when you choose online matches, Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay) assign an anonymous player ID and connect you to other players. Your in-game name and match results are shared with players in the same match and on leaderboards.
  • Analytics: only if you accept the analytics prompt, Unity Analytics receives gameplay events (for example level start, level end, purchases made) with an anonymous ID. Declining stops collection and requests deletion of what was collected.
  • Purchases: purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives the transaction and the anonymous Player ID so your purchases and subscription can be verified and restored. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads: the game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are served as non-personalized ads. We do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and do not access the advertising identifier for tracking. AdMob may process device information and IP address to deliver ads and prevent fraud; see Google’s privacy policy.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, reminders are scheduled on your device. No push token is stored by us.
  • Photos: clips are saved to your photo library only when you tap Save; the game cannot read your photos.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Players whose age is unknown are treated with child-safe settings: non-personalized ads and no pop-up offers.

Retention and deletion

Cloud saves are kept while the account is active and deleted 24 months after the last session, or within 30 days after you ask us at the address above (include the Player ID shown in Profile). Analytics data is kept by Unity for up to 13 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA) you may ask for access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, and you may object to processing. Write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Third parties

Apple (App Store, StoreKit), RevenueCat Inc., Unity Technologies (Gaming Services, Analytics), Google LLC (AdMob), Supabase Inc. Each processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell personal data.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above.
