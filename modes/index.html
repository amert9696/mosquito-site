Game modes in Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run

The game has five modes: a 20-night story, a Daily Night that is the same for every player, a weekly ranked room called Game of the Week, an endless Sleepless Night and Online 1 vs 3. The Daily, Weekly and endless modes open after you win night 03. All five are free.

Story: one summer, twenty nights

The campaign follows one mosquito born in a bathroom drain in June, with one summer to live. It runs in four chapters of five nights: The House, After Hours, On the Road and Nightlife. Night 10 (Racket Uncle) and night 20 (Pest Control) are boss nights. Each night is dated on a summer calendar and adds one new idea, so the game teaches itself as you go.

Daily Night: the same room for everyone

Every day at 00:00 UTC, every player gets the same room with the same two mutators. The mutators are picked from six: Drafty (gusts of wind), Failing bulbs (lights flicker), Lighter sleep, Smaller sips, Wide golden moment and Early dawn. Winning pays 150 Blood, plus 25 for each day in your streak, up to 300 after a week.

Game of the Week: one ranked room

From Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday, everyone plays the same room. Only won nights count. The board ranks by stars first, then by time. The player who leads when the week ends becomes the champion and receives the Gold Champion body, which is never sold.

Sleepless Night: the endless mode

There is no dawn and no window. You stay in the first bedroom and drink for as long as you survive, while the sleeper sleeps more lightly every few minutes. Every 90 seconds the night mutates, alternating a gift for you and a curse on the room:

  • Gifts: Long needle (the golden moment lasts twice as long), Thirst (every sip pays 20% more), Silent wings (they hear and see you less).
  • Curses: Light sleepers, Failing bulbs, A draft.

Your score is the blood you drank.

Online 1 vs 3: one human, three mosquitoes

A match lasts three minutes. The three mosquitoes win by landing 7 bites before time runs out. The human wins by lasting the three minutes, using slaps, bug spray and the lamp. You choose your side before you search.

  • Play with friends: open a private room and share its code. When you press start, bots take the empty seats.
  • Real players pay more: a room full of bots pays 40% of the rewards; a full room of people pays 120%. Your first win of the day against real players gives double XP and 100 Blood.
  • Ranks: Copper, Silver, Gold, Obsidian, Crimson and Apex.
  • No paid advantage: species are cosmetic online.
  • No connection? If the servers cannot be reached, the match runs against bots on your phone.
