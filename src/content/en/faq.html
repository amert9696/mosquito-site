Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run is free on iPhone (iOS 15 or later), has optional in-app purchases, plays offline once a room is downloaded, and has no announced Android release date. The answers below were checked on 24 November 2026.

Is Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run free?

Yes. The download is free, and the whole story and all five modes can be played without paying. Optional in-app purchases are listed below.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 15.0 or later. The game is built for iPhone and plays in landscape. The download is about 393 MB, and some rooms download the first time you play them.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. An Android version is in development, but no release date has been announced. This page will be updated when there is one.

Can I play offline?

Mostly. Story nights, the Daily Night and the endless Sleepless Night work offline once their room has been downloaded. The Game of the Week needs a connection to post your run to the board. Online 1 vs 3 needs a connection to play with real people; without one, it runs against bots on your phone.

Which languages is the game in?

English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Arabic. The game uses your device language and falls back to English.

Blood Sacs, the game's reward boxes, are never sold: you earn them by playing, and the odds are shown in the game.

Does the game have ads?

There are no banner ads and no forced ads. Where a rewarded video is offered, for example for bonus Blood, watching it is always your choice. No Ads and the Mosquito Club turn those rewards into instant ones.

Can players buy an advantage online?

No. In Online 1 vs 3, species are cosmetic, so a paid species plays exactly like a free one.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Non-consumable items, No Ads and the Mosquito Club come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Blood packs and other consumables cannot be restored.

How do I cancel the Mosquito Club?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mosquito. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an account?

No. The game creates an anonymous Player ID for cloud saves and leaderboards, and it never asks for your name, email or phone number. You can choose a display name in Settings if you want to appear on the boards.

How do I delete my data?

In the game, open Settings → Delete account (this needs an internet connection). Or email us with the subject “Delete my data” and the Player ID shown under Profile; we delete your cloud save and profile within 30 days. Purchases stay with your Apple Account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is it suitable for children?

The App Store rates it 9+ for mild cartoon violence and mild horror, and the game is not directed at children under 13. Players whose age is unknown get child-safe settings: non-personalised ads and no pop-up offers. Parents can use Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time to control purchases. Online matches have no free-text chat, only a few preset quick lines such as “Nice bite!”.

How do I play with friends?

Open Online, create a private room and share its code. Friends join with the code; when you start, bots take any empty seats. How online works.

Who makes the game?

Ali Mert Güleç, an independent developer. Press can find facts and images in the press kit; players can reach us on the support page.
