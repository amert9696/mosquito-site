What do you do in a night?

Each night is one room and one goal: drink enough blood to open the window, then get out before dawn. A typical night takes a few minutes.

  1. Sneak in. Hold to fly, drag to steer. Stay out of the light and away from open eyes.
  2. Find skin. Ride the warm plume of a sleeper's breath: it leads you to skin and fills you faster.
  3. Bite. Hold the BITE button and let go just before the ring turns red. Let go when it glows gold for a perfect bite.
  4. Escape. A full belly makes you heavy and slow. Dodge the slap, then fly out of the open window.

What makes it different from other mosquito games?

Many mosquito games on phones are short arcade loops. This one is a stealth game built around five systems that shape every night:

  • Breath plumes: sleepers breathe out visible warm trails. Fly inside one and you lock onto their scent, just as real mosquitoes follow exhaled CO₂.
  • Blood weight: every sip makes you heavier and slower, so the trip back to the window gets harder.
  • Slap Time: when a hand comes down, time slows. Dash clear at the last moment and the game saves the clip for you.
  • Live buzz music: there is no music track. A synth plays your flight and darkens when the room starts to notice you.
  • Real species: ten real mosquitoes, from the house mosquito to the elephant mosquito, each with a bonus based on its biology.

Purchases did not arrive

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Non-consumable items, No Ads and the Mosquito Club come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Blood packs and other consumables cannot be restored.

Cancel the Mosquito Club
