Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run has a 20-night story across 20 realistic rooms, five game modes, ten real mosquito species and an online mode in which one human faces three mosquitoes. It is free to play: purchases are optional, species give no advantage online, and Blood Sacs (the game's reward boxes) are earned by playing and never sold.

Which rooms are in the game?

The story is one mosquito's summer, from June to September, in four chapters of five nights. Each room is a real place at insect scale, with real furniture and people who sleep, stir, talk and work.

What are the signature systems?

SystemWhat it does
Breath plumes and scent lockSleepers breathe out visible warm trails. Ride one to lock onto the scent: it leads to skin and pays up to 50% more blood.
Blood weightEvery sip makes you heavier. Your belly swells, your flight slows and the window feels further away.
Golden biteLet go of the bite just before the itch for 25% more blood, shown in slow motion.
Slap TimeTime slows when a hand comes down. Dash clear and the game saves the near miss as a clip.
Live buzz musicNo music track: a synth plays your flight. Fast and high sounds different from slow and low, and the music darkens when the room notices you.
Compound-eye frameThe edges of the screen react to whoever is watching you, like a mosquito's eyes.

How do the people behave?

People are not targets on rails. They breathe, snore, roll over, sneeze, wake up, search the room with their eyes and fight back with slaps, bug spray and, on night 10, an electric racket. Hiding on a dark wall works because they really look for you.

What can you collect?

  • 10 real mosquito species, each with strengths and trade-offs based on its biology. See all ten.
  • 12 pieces of gear with real effects: wings, needles, a carbon carapace, honey and jet sacs, night lenses.
  • Skins, wings, needles and trails to change how your mosquito looks.
  • Night Pass: every season has a free track and an optional premium track of rewards.
  • Blood Sacs earned by playing, with the odds shown in the game. They are never sold.
  • Mosquito Journal, daily missions and a nest colony to grow over the summer.

Can you play with friends?

Yes. In Online 1 vs 3, one player is the human and three are mosquitoes. Open a private room and share the code; empty seats fill with bots, so a match always starts. Read how every mode works.

Is it fair to free players?

  • The whole story, the Daily, Weekly, endless and online modes are free.
  • Online, species are cosmetic: nobody can buy an advantage.
  • No banner ads and no forced ads.
  • Prices are shown by the App Store in your currency before you confirm. See the FAQ for the full list.

Accessibility and languages

Settings include one-finger, two-thumb and classic controls, left-handed layout, reduced motion, a colour-blind thermal palette, gyro assist, haptics and separate music and sound volumes. The game is translated into English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Arabic.
