Playing as an insect is a small but long-running idea in games: Mister Mosquito put players in a mosquito's wings on PlayStation 2 in 2001, and since then there have been bee, ant and bug games on PC and console, mosquito games on phones and Roblox, and a one-human-versus-four-mosquitoes party game on Steam (2025). Below are notable examples with platform, year and what you actually do. Dates were checked on 24 November 2026.

What do insect games have in common?

Three things come up again and again. Scale: an ordinary room becomes a huge level, which is the whole appeal of Mister Mosquito and of our own rooms. Stealth: a mosquito or a bee is fragile, so the tension comes from not being noticed rather than from fighting. Comedy: annoying a giant human is funny, which is why mosquito and hornet games on phones lean into slapstick.

Which games let you play as a mosquito?

Mister Mosquito is the classic: a 2001 PS2 stealth game set in one family's house that sold 41,006 copies in Japan in its launch week, according to Famitsu figures cited by Wikipedia, and returned on PS4 and PS5 in 2024. On phones, Mosquito io (Supercent, 2022) has about 45,000 ratings on the US App Store; Moshquito! (Lion Studios, 2021) is another (iTunes Search API, 24 November 2026). On Roblox, Feed A Mosquito passed 50 million visits in 2026. Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run is a newer, more realistic take: real mosquito species and real rooms.

Are there games where one player is the human?

Yes. The Mosquito Gang on Steam (Space Raccoon Game Studio, 2025) has an asymmetric mode in which one player is the human doing chores and four players are mosquitoes with powers such as invisibility. Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run has the same idea on iPhone with three mosquitoes: Online 1 vs 3 gives the human three minutes to survive and the mosquitoes need seven bites.

How should you choose?

  • Want a story on console: Bug Fables, or Mister Mosquito on PS4/PS5.
  • Want strategy: Empire of the Ants.
  • Want a calm open world: Bee Simulator.
  • Want a party game with friends on PC: The Mosquito Gang.
  • Want stealth on iPhone, solo or online: Mosquito Simulator: Bite & Run. See how to play.
